Aligned with UN SDGs

SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being

Active & Healthy Lifestyle

Encouraging students to replace short-distance scooter or car rides with walking, running, and cycling to improve cardiovascular health and reduce daily stress.

SDG 13: Climate Action

Measurable Carbon Reduction

Every kilometer moved without fossil fuels and every single-use plastic cup avoided directly lowers carbon emissions. We turn small daily habits into visible climate impact.
